## Liquid pump direction and pressure limitation

`liquid_pump.parse_save_data` at 56363657 binds `content_0`, `content_1`, `is_reverse`, and `flow_factor`. Their literal names and default reverse=false/flow_factor=1 are recorded in evidence. The corresponding fields are at component offsets +0x1e0, +0x2c8, +0x440, and +0x448 respectively.

`liquid_pump.tick` at 56368934 checks that both liquid nodes exist before active pumping. It multiplies shaft RPS by +1 or -1 according to `is_reverse`; the resulting sign selects transfer direction between the two chambers. Thus reverse changes the effective shaft sign, while reversing shaft rotation also changes direction.

The reviewed arithmetic can be expressed with local names:

```text
s = shaft_rps * (is_reverse ? -1 : +1)
H = abs(s) * flow_factor * 0.1
loss = clamp(max(destination_pressure - source_pressure, 0)
             / max(H, 0.0001), 0, 1)
attempt = min(source_chamber_quantity, H * 0.4 * server_delta_time)
requested_transfer = attempt * (1 - loss)
```

For nonnegative `flow_factor` and finite ordinary inputs, increasing opposing pressure reduces the requested transfer; sufficiently large opposition reduces it to zero. The path also sets opposite static pressures on the two nodes and applies a shaft load. The transfer helper and surrounding network determine actual end-to-end movement; this expression alone is not a complete circulation simulation.

Constants 0.1, 0.0001, 0.4, -1, +1, and 0.6 are byte-checked. The 0.6 appears in load/power arithmetic; an efficiency interpretation remains an inference. This checkpoint does not assign guessed SI units to `H` or turn the expression into an unsupported universal L/s rating. Geometry, connection state, source contents, pressure, and shaft direction still matter.

## Microcontroller power and execution

`microcontroller.tick` at 56807623 uses a **10.0 base demand**, multiplies it by server delta time, and requests a supplied fraction through the connected electrical node. An unconnected group leaves the fraction at zero. Reported power is fraction * 10; the data-node distribute-power flag is enabled only when fraction > **0.01**.

The execution branch also requires supplied fraction >0.01 and other controller-state flags. Therefore full 10 W supply is not the numeric condition for entering this branch; power alone does not guarantee a valid, enabled, running program. Exactly 1% does not pass the strict comparison.

The disabled/unpowered branch iterates controller values and resets the analogue subtype through `vehicle_data_analogue.reset` at 58131605. This is evidence about those controller values, not proof that all global state or connected physical actuators reset.

`server_scene_script.element.run` at 59468029 dispatches normal elements, while a flagged element stops its context and stores the cursor. `element.step`, parser entry points, and cursor-start logic were also exported. Full scheduling, instruction budgets, grammar, and persistence remain incomplete; no execution-rate limit is claimed from these excerpts.
